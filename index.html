VAPOR

Ship nothing. Scale everything.

A satirical startup simulator. You will not learn real business here.

How to play

  • Goal: survive 2 years of sprints and reach a good ending.
  • Each sprint: pick 1 of 4 feature cards, split your budget across Engineering / Marketing / Support, then resolve a random event.
  • Watch: Cash, MRR, Users, Churn, Reputation, Burn rate.
  • Endings: IPO, Acquired, Bootstrapped (win), Bankrupt, Scandal (lose).
  • Keyboard: 1-4 pick feature, ←/→ adjust budget, Space confirm, P pause, R restart, M mute, ? help, T theme.
  • Touch: tap cards, tap +/- budget, tap Confirm.
  • Determinism: same seed = same event deck and rival moves.
